## Scientific and Academic Engagement

Beyond my research and teaching activities, I am actively involved in the scientific community, academic life, mentoring, scientific outreach, and the structuring of additive manufacturing networks.

My engagement combines **scientific expertise, academic responsibilities, community building, mentoring, outreach, and the promotion of women in science and engineering**.

### Scientific Community

I contribute to the structuring and animation of the additive manufacturing and robotics communities at regional, national, and international levels.

I am **co-scientific lead of the MAD4AM cluster**, together with two colleagues. The cluster brings together academic and industrial stakeholders working on additive manufacturing, advanced manufacturing, and future construction.

We organize **several scientific and industrial events every year**. Since **2025**, we have also organized the **“Midis de la Fabrication Additive”**, a monthly online scientific seminar held on the **first Monday of each month**, usually gathering **25 to 50 participants per session** from academia and industry.

I am also an active member of the international **RILEM** community, particularly on topics related to earthen materials, digital construction, and sustainable manufacturing processes.

I contribute to scientific evaluation activities through:
- participation in **ANR evaluation committees CES10 and CES51**;
- evaluation of **5 ANR proposals** over the recent period;
- peer review for international journals including *Additive Manufacturing* and *Rapid Prototyping Journal*;
- review of **8 international conference papers**, including contributions for ISARC, CableCon and Manufacturing 21.

I have also contributed to the organization of several scientific events, including:
- **Metal Additive Manufacturing Technical Day**, 28 March 2024, approximately **150 participants**;
- **Journées Nationales de l’Enseignement en Robotique (JNER)**, June 2025, approximately **100 participants**;
- **CL14 – “From the Cell to the House: Scientific Continuities in Additive Manufacturing in Nantes”**, Nantes Université Scientific Days, **29 May 2026**.

### Academic Responsibilities

Since **2024**, I have been **coordinator of the mentoring programme for newly appointed Associate Professors at Nantes Université**, together with my colleague **Mathilde Lancelot**.

We jointly coordinate the programme at university level, including the creation and follow-up of mentor–mentee pairs, support for newly recruited academics, and the organization of dedicated events and discussion sessions.

The programme represents approximately **30 mentor–mentee pairs per year** across Nantes Université. I have also personally supported several newly appointed academics through individual mentoring.

I am an **elected member of the LS2N Laboratory Council** and contribute to the collective scientific and organizational life of the laboratory.

Since **2025**, I have also been an **elected member of the Board of France Additive**, where I contribute to the structuring of the French additive manufacturing community and to the preparation of the **France Additive Forum 2027 in Nantes**.

My academic service also includes participation in recruitment and evaluation procedures. Since 2021, I have participated in **doctoral thesis committees and national recruitment committees for Associate Professors**, including committees in mechanical engineering, robotics and computer science.

### Science and Society

A significant part of my engagement is dedicated to scientific outreach, science education and the promotion of women in science and engineering.

Since **2025**, I have been an ambassador for **“La Science Taille XX Elles”**, an initiative supported by the **CNRS Pays de la Loire** to increase the visibility of women scientists and encourage young women to consider scientific and engineering careers.

My activities include regular outreach actions for middle-school pupils, high-school pupils and students, through laboratory visits, scientific events and career-orientation activities.

I also participate in the **Gender Equality working group of the Nantes Education Authority**. In **February 2026**, I gave a presentation to approximately **250 inspectors and education professionals** on the orientation of girls towards science, engineering and technical careers.

Since **2021**, I have regularly participated in outreach events such as:
- **Fête de la Science**;
- **Nuit des Chercheurs**;
- **CNRS “Échappées inattendues”**;
- laboratory visits;
- interventions in technological and vocational high schools;
- workshops on robotics, additive manufacturing and sustainable construction.

These actions have reached **several hundred young people**.

My engagement in this area follows a long-term trajectory:
- **2009** – recipient of the *Prix de la vocation scientifique et technique des filles*;
- **2019** – recognized in the *Femmes dans la Construction* initiative;
- **2025** – ambassador for *La Science Taille XX Elles* – CNRS Pays de la Loire.

### Professional Networks

I am involved in several national and international networks that connect research, industry, education and additive manufacturing.

My main engagements include:
- **France Additive** – elected Board member since 2025;
- **MAD4AM** – co-scientific lead;
- **RILEM** – active member of international scientific committees;
- **Women in 3D Printing** – member of the international network promoting women in additive manufacturing;
- scientific collaborations with academic and industrial partners in France and abroad.

These activities are part of a broader objective: **building stronger links between research, industry, education and society, while making scientific and engineering careers more visible and accessible to younger generations.**
