# Elodie Paquet Tessier

**Associate Professor in Mechanical Engineering**  
Nantes Université · IUT de Nantes  
LS2N UMR CNRS 6004 · RoMaS Team

My research activities focus on **robotic additive manufacturing, large-scale manufacturing, artificial intelligence, computer vision, process monitoring and digital twins**, with applications to advanced manufacturing and sustainable construction.

---

## Current Positions & Responsibilities

- **Associate Professor in Mechanical Engineering** — Nantes Université, IUT de Nantes, LS2N UMR CNRS 6004.
- **Director of Studies, Master ITI** — Nantes Université, since 2024.
- **Principal Investigator, ANR JCJC SmartAMP** — 2024–2029.
- **Scientific Lead, Ecobrick 4.0**.
- **Scientific Lead, ITA — Inclusivité en Terre Additive**.
- **Co-Scientific Lead, MAD4AM Cluster**.
